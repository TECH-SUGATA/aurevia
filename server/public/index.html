About the project

The problem

Most houseplants die from too much or too little water, and diseases are noticed late. Aurevia turns sensor readings and leaf photos into simple, timely advice.

The technology

A Raspberry Pi with soil sensors sends readings to the Aurevia server. Rules and AI check them against healthy ranges, trigger the pump when needed, and a vision model reviews leaf photos for disease.
